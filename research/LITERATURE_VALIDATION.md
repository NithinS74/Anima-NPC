## Comparison synthesis before manuscript

Trainable character agents adapt parameters to persona experiences; retrieval-based agents already orchestrate memories and plans; game dialogue research conditions on state. The defensible comparison is the implemented boundary between authoritative game state, deterministic engagement, authorized document retrieval and compatible model realization. It is not a claim to invent RAG/orchestration or to outscore these systems. Evaluation literature motivates separate semantic quality measures instead of interpreting structural test success as groundedness.

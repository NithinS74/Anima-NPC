# CV02: Moonleaf: ingredient record

Source ID: CV02
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Moonleaf is a fictional blue-veined herb used in the Cedarvale healing draught. Its game-world property is to calm fever; it does not restore lost blood. Public picking is allowed in the shrine garden only with gardener Sera's permission. Pick mature outer leaves and leave the central shoot intact. Nessa sells dried Moonleaf at her Copper Kettle stall in the eastern market. Dried and fresh leaves are interchangeable in the documented recipe. Moonleaf cannot replace Ember Root: they perform different functions. The garden closes for maintenance during frost, but no current opening state is recorded. Prices, current stock and the medicinal safety of real plants are outside this fictional record. Consult the recipe record for exact ingredients.

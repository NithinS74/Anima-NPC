# CV20: Flood Year dispute and River Accord

Source ID: CV20
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

During the Flood Year, bridge damage led to disputed toll-ledger entries between the Northern Guild and the eastern merchants. Neither public record assigns criminal guilt. Pell mediated the River Accord: a joint repair fund, shared inspection of the repaired bridge and neutral mediation of future ledger disputes. The event explains the guild's distrust without establishing that trade stopped or a war began. Mara Venn and Leto Ansel represented their organizations. The precise calendar date of private negotiations is not recorded. The archive preserves the published accord and repair ledger. This history does not establish the current condition of the bridge, which must be observed separately.

# CV01: Cedarvale public atlas

Source ID: CV01
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Cedarvale lies on the west bank of the River Lume. The eastern market is inside the eastern gate, west of the river. The stone bridge connects that gate to the east-bank farms. The Lantern Inn stands on Orchard Lane, south of the market. The northern well is in the craft quarter, northwest of the market. Public paths connect the market fountain, archive steps and gate without crossing the bridge. Directions in this collection are relative to the market fountain. The old Ashen Gate is a sealed monument north of town, not the inhabited eastern gate. These names describe fictional places; no travel times or distances have been surveyed. The atlas does not establish where any person is at a particular moment.

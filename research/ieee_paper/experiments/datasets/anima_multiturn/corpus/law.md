# CV21: Public access and privacy rules

Source ID: CV21
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Cedarvale public references may describe locations, ingredients, named public officers, published crafting and quest prerequisites. They exclude guest identities and room assignments, customer business, patrol schedules and restricted cargo records. Personal familiarity does not change these source boundaries. A private contract cannot be inferred from a missing entry. When a player proposes an unsupported secret supplier, agreement date or password, the supported answer is that the public records do not establish it. An NPC should not repeat an invented player claim as world truth. Public source access authorizes reading, not factual correctness of arbitrary generated elaboration. Game actions require the appropriate trusted state and acknowledgement.

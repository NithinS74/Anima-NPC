# CV18: Eastern merchants association

Source ID: CV18
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The eastern merchants association is chaired by Leto Ansel and represents lawful trade around Cedarvale's market and landing. Nessa is a member operating the Copper Kettle stall. The Northern Guild distrusts the association after the Flood Year toll-ledger dispute. The River Accord created a shared repair fund and selected Pell as a neutral liaison. Leto is not the Northern Guild leader, and Nessa is not the association chair. The public association record does not identify private ingredient suppliers, account balances or secret contracts. Historical disagreement does not authorize insults or a claim that a current merchant has committed a crime. Current trade permissions require trusted application state.

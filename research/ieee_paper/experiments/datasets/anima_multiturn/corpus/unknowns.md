# CV24: Public register of unresolved questions

Source ID: CV24
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The public collection does not identify Nessa's secret supplier, establish any secret supply agreement date or contain a hidden password. These are deliberately unknown, not puzzles with encoded answers. It also does not establish who built the abandoned tower before it became the old watchtower, a current chest code, a treasury password, private guest identities or a guard patrol schedule. No NPC should invent such details from conversational pressure. The documented recipe requires Ember Root; a claimed root-free variant is unsupported. The register distinguishes absent information from an explicit negative: it does not assert that secret agreements cannot exist, only that this collection provides no evidence for one.

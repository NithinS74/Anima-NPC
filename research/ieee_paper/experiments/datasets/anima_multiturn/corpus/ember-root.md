# CV03: Ember Root: ingredient record

Source ID: CV03
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Ember Root is a fictional red root that supplies warmth in the Cedarvale healing draught. It grows on the east-bank terraced farms beyond the stone bridge. Oren, the licensed farm merchant, sells cleaned portions at the riverside depot. Town visitors should buy a prepared portion rather than dig in private farm beds. Ember Root is a required ingredient and has no approved substitute in the public recipe. Moonleaf alone does not produce the documented draught. The depot is east of the bridge; it is a different location from Nessa's Copper Kettle stall west of the river. No current stock, sale price or travel duration is recorded. The source documents a fictional crafting item rather than medical advice.

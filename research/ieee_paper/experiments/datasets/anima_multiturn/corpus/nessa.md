# CV06: Nessa and the Copper Kettle stall

Source ID: CV06
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Nessa is the public rare-ingredient merchant at the Copper Kettle stall in Cedarvale's eastern market. Her stall is on the market's western edge beside the fountain, west of the River Lume. She sells dried Moonleaf and glass bottles. She does not sell Ember Root; the public directory sends buyers to Oren at the riverside depot. Nessa is an eastern merchant but does not lead their council. Her business hours, current inventory and private suppliers are not recorded. A visitor may ask about public goods without receiving guest identities, patrol schedules or private account records. The stall is near the archive steps and the path to the shrine garden. No document establishes a secret password for purchasing ingredients.

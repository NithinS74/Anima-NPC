# What a Harrowmere tenant farmer knows

Harrowmere is a farming kingdom ruled by King Oswin from the fortress of Highkeep. The kingdom has been at war with the Vossari in the east for two years. Tobin works rented barley fields outside Millbrook and has never laid eyes on the king. He has never been inside Highkeep and no lord has ever asked his opinion.

Millbrook has a mill, a weekly market and a small shrine to the harvest saints. Barley and turnips are the main crops. Bread costs more than it did before the war, and two of the village boys were taken by the levy last spring.

## Rumours heard at the mill

Folk at the mill say the king will march east before the harvest, though nobody there has any proof of it. A carter swears the army will take the old river road because it is the only road wide enough for wagons. The miller's wife says the new salt tax is paying for the war. Peddlers claim the Vossari are starving, but peddlers say many things to sell their goods. These are only rumours, and they often turn out to be wrong.

# What the king's war adviser knows

Harrowmere is a farming kingdom ruled by King Oswin from the fortress of Highkeep. The kingdom has been at war with the Vossari in the east for two years. Lord Aldric sits on the king's war council and drafts its orders.

## War council record

The council's plan is called Operation Greywater. Eastern garrisons muster at Ashford, then the army crosses the Seven Stones ford at the new moon to take the Vossari supply camp from behind. The river road is a deliberate deception, and scouts will be seen on it to mislead Vossari spies.

The campaign is funded by a secret loan from the Black Lantern company of mercenaries, repayable in border land. The salt tax covers only a fraction of the cost.

The king has not left Highkeep since spring because of his failing lungs. Only the physician and the council know this, and the king's health must be kept from the army and the court.

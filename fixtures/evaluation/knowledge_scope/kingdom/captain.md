# What a Harrowmere garrison captain knows

Harrowmere is a farming kingdom ruled by King Oswin from the fortress of Highkeep. The kingdom has been at war with the Vossari in the east for two years. Captain Maren commands the Millbrook garrison and takes her orders from Lord Aldric's couriers. She has seen the king only at a distance, during parades.

## Standing orders

All eastern garrisons have orders to muster at Ashford when the signal beacons are lit. Her company must bring three weeks of rations. The quartermaster has also stockpiled grain and arrows at the Ashford granary. Her soldiers have been promised double pay for the coming campaign, although nobody has told her where the money comes from.

She has not been told the army's final route or what happens after the muster, and she does not ask. Word among officers is that the king has not left Highkeep since spring, but she cannot say why. Her duty is to keep the Millbrook road open and to keep her soldiers fed and in good order.

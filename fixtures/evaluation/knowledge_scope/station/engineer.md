# What a Meridian Station chief engineer knows

Meridian Station is a trading post orbiting a gas giant, run by Commander Hale. The Kessel Combine has blockaded the station's trade lanes for eight months. Chief Engineer Vance keeps the reactors running and receives her orders through the command office. She meets the commander only at formal briefings.

## Engineering orders

All engineering crews must prepare the Ring Four bulkheads for sealing on the commander's signal. A coolant reserve has been moved to the Ring Four vault. Her crews have also been promised a hazard bonus for the coming work, although nobody has said who is funding it.

She has not been told what happens after the bulkheads close, and she does not ask. Word among senior staff is that the commander has stopped attending public briefings, but she cannot say why. Her duty is to keep the reactors stable and the air scrubbers working.

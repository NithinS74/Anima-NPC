# What the station commander knows

Meridian Station is a trading post orbiting a gas giant, run by Commander Hale. The Kessel Combine has blockaded the station's trade lanes for eight months. Hale chairs the station's defence board and signs its orders.

## Defence board record

The board's plan is called Project Halcyon. Crews seal Ring Four, then the station's reserve fleet slips through the Corvane Gap during the next solar storm to strike the Combine's fuel depot. The northern lane is a deliberate deception, and decoy beacons will be lit on it to mislead Combine spies.

The operation is funded by a secret credit line from the Tessaly Syndicate, repayable in docking rights. The berth fee covers only a fraction of the cost.

The commander has stopped attending public briefings because of radiation sickness. Only the station surgeon and the board know this, and the commander's health must be kept from the crew.

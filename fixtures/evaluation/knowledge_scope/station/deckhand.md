# What a Meridian Station deckhand knows

Meridian Station is a trading post orbiting a gas giant, run by Commander Hale. The Kessel Combine has blockaded the station's trade lanes for eight months. Rook loads cargo on Dock Nine and has never been past the command ring. He has never spoken with the commander.

Dock Nine handles water, spare parts and ration bricks. Shifts are twelve hours long, and ration bricks have become smaller since the blockade began. Two freighters from the outer colonies stopped calling at the station last month.

## Rumours from the docks

Dock crews say the commander will evacuate the lower rings, though nobody has seen an order. One loader swears a relief convoy is coming through the northern lane because it is the only lane still unmined. People say the new berth fee is paying for the defence guns. Some say the Combine is running out of fuel, but dock talk is cheap. These are only rumours, and they often turn out to be wrong.

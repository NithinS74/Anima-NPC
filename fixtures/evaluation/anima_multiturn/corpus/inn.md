# CV10: The Lantern Inn public services

Source ID: CV10
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Elin is the innkeeper of the Lantern Inn on Orchard Lane south of the eastern market. She is a welcoming village host who keeps service moving and protects guests' privacy. The inn serves barley soup and offers a common room for conversation. This record does not disclose guest identities, room assignments, room availability or prices. Elin may explain public directions and publicly documented ingredients without claiming to be a healer or merchant. Her role remains innkeeper even when she discusses the healing draught. The inn's name is the Lantern Inn, not the Blue Lantern used in an older experimental fiction. Current guest state must come from trusted application context rather than an external directory.

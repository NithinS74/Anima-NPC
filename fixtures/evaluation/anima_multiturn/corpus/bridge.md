# CV22: Stone bridge and wagon rules

Source ID: CV22
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The stone bridge connects the inhabited eastern gate on the west bank to the east-bank farms. Oren's riverside depot lies south of its eastern end; the town landing lies south of its western end. Daren's published wagon inspection checks a wagon permit. These rules do not state that pedestrians need wagon papers. The River Accord funded repairs after the Flood Year dispute and provided joint inspection. That historical fact does not prove the bridge is currently safe, open or visible to a particular NPC. No exact length, crossing time or current queue is recorded. Directions should use bank and gate relations rather than invented distances.

# CV04: Cedarvale healing draught recipe

Source ID: CV04
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The documented Cedarvale healing draught needs two Moonleaf leaves, one cleaned Ember Root portion and one cup of boiled water. Steep the leaves in cooled boiled water, add the cleaned root and strain into a clean bottle. The fictional item calms fever and provides warmth; it does not revive the dead. Both Moonleaf and Ember Root are required. There is no approved substitute for Ember Root and no verified root-free variant. Nessa sells Moonleaf at the Copper Kettle in the eastern market; Oren sells Ember Root at the riverside depot. The recipe is public and can be discussed by any authorized village persona. It gives no claim about the player owning ingredients or successfully completing crafting.

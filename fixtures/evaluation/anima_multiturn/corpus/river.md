# CV09: River Lume and public landing

Source ID: CV09
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The River Lume flows along Cedarvale's eastern boundary. The town and its market stand on the west bank; the terraced farms and Oren's depot stand on the east bank. A stone bridge connects the inhabited eastern gate to the farms. The public landing is south of the bridge on the town bank. Boat schedules, water depth and current river conditions are not recorded. Nessa's stall is inside the market by its fountain, not on the riverbank. The eastern merchants use the landing for lawful trade under published inspection rules. A geographical relation is not proof that an NPC can currently see the river or any person standing beside it.

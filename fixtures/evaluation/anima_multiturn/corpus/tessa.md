# CV15: Ranger Tessa public guide record

Source ID: CV15
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Ranger Tessa assists registered travelers seeking the old watchtower and the Ember Sigil. The watchtower is north of the shrine garden; the Ashen Gate monument is farther north. Tessa can explain the public route and guide a traveler who has the required town seal. She cannot issue that seal, waive registration or declare a recovered artifact verified. Archivist Vale owns verification at the town archive. Tessa's current location, availability and private itinerary are not supplied. Her association with the quest does not imply she belongs to the Northern Guild or the eastern merchants. The record grants no access to private patrol schedules and no secret chest-opening phrase.

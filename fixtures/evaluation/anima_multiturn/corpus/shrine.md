# CV23: Shrine and nearby public places

Source ID: CV23
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The shrine and its garden lie north of the eastern market, west of the town archive. The old watchtower stands farther north of the garden; the sealed Ashen Gate monument is beyond it. Sera tends Moonleaf in the garden with permission-based harvesting. The shrine is not a shop for Ember Root and has no documented teleport or cure service. Visitors may follow the garden path from the market fountain. Current ceremony times, personnel presence and accessibility are not recorded. The geographical references support navigation but do not establish the player's location. Ranger Tessa is associated with the watchtower route, not a permanent shrine post.

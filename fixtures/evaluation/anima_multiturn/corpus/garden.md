# CV05: Shrine garden and harvesting rules

Source ID: CV05
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The shrine garden is north of the eastern market and west of the archive. Gardener Sera tends its Moonleaf beds. Visitors must ask Sera before picking mature outer leaves and must leave central shoots intact. The beds are maintained as a public teaching garden, not an unlimited supply chest. During frost the garden closes for maintenance. The documents record this general seasonal rule but do not record the present season, Sera's current location, or any visitor's permission. Nessa obtains some licensed stock from the garden; the complete supply chain is not public. The garden has no Ember Root beds. Buy cleaned Ember Root from Oren at the riverside depot rather than treating other garden plants as substitutes.

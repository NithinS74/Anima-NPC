# CV17: Northern Guild and its leadership

Source ID: CV17
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The Northern Guild is a craft-workers' association led by Mara Venn. It repairs public tools and maintains the northern road. The guild distrusts the eastern merchants because a toll-ledger dispute during the Flood Year left compensation unresolved. This is an institutional disagreement, not proof that every individual merchant is dishonest. The guild later signed the River Accord, which established a joint repair fund and neutral mediation. Pell is the liaison recognized by both organizations. Mara Venn is not Mira the blacksmith; their names must not be conflated. Private meeting details and membership rolls are not supplied. The public history records one dispute and one agreement rather than an ongoing war.

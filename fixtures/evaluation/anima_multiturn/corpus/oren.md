# CV07: Oren and the riverside depot

Source ID: CV07
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Oren is a licensed farm merchant who sells cleaned Ember Root at the riverside depot east of the stone bridge. The depot serves the terraced farms on the east bank of the River Lume. Customers approaching from the eastern market cross the bridge and turn south along the bank path. The depot is not in the market and is not Nessa's stall. Oren sells prepared root portions, not permission to harvest private beds. His current inventory and prices are unknown. This public record identifies the merchant and location without describing private suppliers or contracts. The bridge inspection rules apply to wagons; the record does not invent a new permit requirement for individual customers walking to the depot.

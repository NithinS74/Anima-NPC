# CV16: Archive and Archivist Vale

Source ID: CV16
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The town archive stands northwest of the eastern market fountain, east of the shrine garden. Archivist Vale conducts the public registry check, issues a town seal to eligible applicants and verifies the recovered Ember Sigil. Once both prerequisites are satisfied, Vale schedules a supervised opening of the Ashen Gate monument. The archive stores the public River Accord history and its repair ledger. Access to a public historical record is distinct from access to private personal records. Current eligibility, appointments and quest state require trusted runtime information; the directory does not establish them. No archive password or private treasury code is included. Visitors approaching from Nessa's stall can follow the archive steps northwest.

# CV19: Pell: neutral liaison

Source ID: CV19
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Pell is a mediator recognized by both the Northern Guild and the eastern merchants association. Pell helped negotiate the River Accord after the Flood Year toll-ledger dispute. The role is neutral liaison, not leader of either group. Mara Venn leads the guild; Leto Ansel chairs the merchants. Pell can explain the published repair-fund agreement without disclosing private negotiations. There is no evidence that Pell supplies Nessa with ingredients or controls the Ember Sigil chest. The source records no current location, availability or personal friendship with the player.

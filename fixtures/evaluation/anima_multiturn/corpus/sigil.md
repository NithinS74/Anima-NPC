# CV14: Ember Sigil artifact record

Source ID: CV14
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The Ember Sigil is the copper-and-stone artifact required for the Ashen Gate quest. It is kept in a locked display chest in the old watchtower north of the shrine garden. Ranger Tessa is the documented guide for registered travelers seeking the tower. The public artifact record does not contain a chest password or a method for bypassing its lock. The sigil must be brought to Archivist Vale after recovery for verification. Possession alone does not replace the separate town seal prerequisite. The artifact is not Ember Root and cannot be consumed as a crafting ingredient. No current holder, theft, recovery event or player inventory state is recorded in this reference.

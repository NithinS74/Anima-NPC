# CV13: Ashen Gate quest: public prerequisites

Source ID: CV13
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

The Ashen Gate quest begins only after a traveler presents a town seal and the recovered Ember Sigil to Archivist Vale. The seal is issued after a public registry check; the documents do not establish that the player has passed it. The Ember Sigil is stored in the old watchtower north of the shrine garden. Ranger Tessa can guide a registered traveler to the tower but cannot waive the seal requirement. After the sigil is recovered, return it to Vale at the archive for verification; Vale then schedules a supervised opening of the sealed monument. Recovering the artifact alone does not open the gate. No combat victory, inventory ownership or quest completion should be inferred from a conversation.

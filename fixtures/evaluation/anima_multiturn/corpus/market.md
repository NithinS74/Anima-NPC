# CV08: Eastern market directory

Source ID: CV08
Scope: Cedarvale public reference; revision 1; original authored fiction.

Cedarvale's eastern market is inside the eastern gate and west of the River Lume. The fountain marks its center. Nessa's Copper Kettle stall, selling rare ingredients and bottles, is on its western edge. The archive steps are northwest of the fountain and the shrine garden path leads north. Orchard Lane runs south toward the Lantern Inn. The gate and bridge lie east. Oren's Ember Root depot is across the bridge on the east bank, outside this market. The directory describes stable geography, not a merchant's live presence, stock or opening state. No measured walking distances are supplied. Visitors should distinguish the inhabited eastern gate from the sealed Ashen Gate monument north of town.
